      <h3 style="font-family:'Source Serif 4',Georgia,serif;font-size:1.9rem;font-weight:500;">Investigating the Bulge Morphology of Dual AGN Host Galaxies from the GOTHIC Survey</h3>
      <p>Study of 131 bulges across dual-AGN systems and triplets using SDSS <i>gri</i> imaging and GALFIT. The work examines Sérsic indices, luminosities, masses, and scale lengths to investigate the structural nature of dual-AGN host galaxies.</p>
