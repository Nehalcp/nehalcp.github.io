      <h1>Learning physics, then learning how to ask astronomical questions.</h1>
      <p class="lede">My path has gradually moved from physics and computational methods toward observational studies of galaxies and active galactic nuclei.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="timeline">
      <div class="timeline-item"><div class="year">2018 — 2023</div><h3>Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Bhopal</h3><p>BS-MS in Physics, with a minor in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. This period built my foundation in physics, computation, and quantitative problem solving.</p></div>
      <div class="timeline-item"><div class="year">2022 — 2023</div><h3>MS Thesis · Automated identification of merging galaxies</h3><p>Worked on the GOTHIC algorithm for identifying double-nucleus systems in SDSS data, followed by manual validation and physical analysis using colour–magnitude and emission-line diagnostics.</p></div>
      <div class="timeline-item"><div class="year">2024 — 2025</div><h3>Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru</h3><p>Developed a research programme around dual AGN host galaxies and their structural properties, using SDSS imaging, GALFIT, photometric decomposition, and Python-based analysis.</p></div>
      <div class="timeline-item"><div class="year">2025</div><h3>First-author MNRAS publication</h3><p>Led a follow-up study of bulge morphology in dual AGN host galaxies, resulting in a first-author paper accepted for publication in MNRAS.</p></div>
      <div class="timeline-item"><div class="year">Present</div><h3>Looking toward the next research chapter</h3><p>Continuing to develop interests in galaxy evolution, AGN, supermassive black holes, and observational techniques that can connect galaxy structure to nuclear activity.</p></div>
